dB HL Hearing Loss Severity Table:

"dB HL = How loud the sound needs to be for the person to hear it."

dB HL Range Hearing Classification Impact on Daily Speech & Sounds
0 – 20 dB HLNormal HearingCan hear soft whispers (<20 dB) effortlessly
21 – 25 dB HLBorderline Hearing LossSlight difficulty with soft speech in background noise
26 – 40 dB HLMild Hearing LossMisses soft consonants (f, s, th); quiet rooms needed
41 – 55 dB HLModerate Hearing LossUnderstands conversational speech only at close range
56 – 70 dB HLModerately Severe LossRequires loud speech; struggles in group conversations
71 – 90 dB HLSevere Hearing LossShouted speech or amplified hearing devices required
90+ dB HLProfound Hearing LossOnly perceives very loud vibrations / environmental signals

Sound Frequency Spectrum Categories:

125 Hz: Low Pitch Sound (Bass, Drums)
250 – 800 Hz: Mid Pitch Sound (Human Voice Vowels)
1,000 – 8,000 Hz: High Pitch Sound (Consonants, Birds, Whistle)
10,000 – 20,000 Hz: Very High Pitch Sound (Ultrasonic / Mosquito Tones)
